## Overview

Creation of a web application for administration and students of the Universidad Juárez de Durango, enabling online payments and payment management for the university.

## Key Features

- **Student Enrollment and Payments**: Students can easily enroll in courses or complete various types of payments through the platform. The system offers multiple payment options, allowing users to pay online with a card, at the bank, or in convenience stores, providing flexibility and accessibility for all students.
- **Comprehensive Payment Tracking**: Administrators have access to detailed payment reports, enabling them to monitor who has completed payments and follow up on each enrollment. This ensures transparency and efficient management of student records and financial transactions.

## Technologies Used

- **Next.js**: For fast, server-rendered React applications.
- **Figma**: For creating and managing design assets and prototypes.
- **Styled-Components**: For styling React components with a modular, themable approach.
- **OpenPay by BBVA**: Utilizado como motor de pago para procesar transacciones de manera segura y eficiente.
- **TailwindCSS**: Para estilos utilitarios rápidos y personalizables, facilitando la creación de interfaces consistentes y responsivas.

## Challenges and Learnings

One of the main challenges was reconciling payments that were either rejected or made independently at the bank, ensuring that all transactions were accurately recorded in the system. Additionally, we had to integrate and learn to use a new payment engine, OpenPay, which required adapting to its documentation, workflows, and technical specifics to guarantee secure and efficient transactions.
